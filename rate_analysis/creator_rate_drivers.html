Reading this

Prices are asking prices. They come from creators' own rate cards, not from closed deals.

A link is not a cause. Large accounts also tend to have more views, so views look related to price mostly because they travel with followers. The grouped bars above separate the two.

Only creators with an engagement rate of 1% or more are included. Creators with no engagement rate, and those below 1%, are left out of every chart on this page. Rates that low are often inactive or inflated accounts. A few remaining values are above 80%, so dots above 30% are left off the engagement chart, but the correlations still use them.

Views and engagement add little beyond followers. Once follower count is known, the grouped bars swing up in some buckets and down in others, so there is no steady premium for higher views or engagement.
