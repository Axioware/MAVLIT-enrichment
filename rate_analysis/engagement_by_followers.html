How this is calculated

Per post: (likes + comments) ÷ the creator's follower count × 100.

Posts used: only posts with more than 1 like and more than 1 comment. Hidden like counts (stored as −1) and posts with no comments are left out. This also removes the very lowest rates, so engagement here is a little higher than the true average.

Per creator: the average (or median) of that creator's qualifying posts. Follower count is the one from the creator's most recent scrape. Creators with no qualifying post are not shown.

Outliers: a viral post on a small account can exceed 100%. The median option and the minimum-posts filter reduce their effect.
