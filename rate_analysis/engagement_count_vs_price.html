Engagement count against followers

Engagement count is the number of engagements a creator gets, worked out as engagement rate × followers ÷ 100. Bigger accounts have more of them, even though their rate is lower.

Reading this

Engagement count is not stored in the CSV. It is calculated for each creator as engagement rate × follower count ÷ 100.

It is built from followers. Because the count contains the follower number, it moves with followers by construction. The row "after followers" on the left removes that overlap.

Same limits as before. Prices are asking prices, and engagement rates in the CSVs include outliers, which is why 1% and above is the default.
